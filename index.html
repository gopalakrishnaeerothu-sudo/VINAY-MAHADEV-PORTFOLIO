VINAY MAHADEV

Theatre Training & Acting Roots

Vinay Mahadev is a seasoned, theatre-trained actor with a rich foundation in dramatic stage craft. Trained through the prestigious National School of Drama (NSD) Theatre Workshop and having performed extensively with Bahurupa Nata Samaikya, Vinay honed his craft in voice modulation, dialogue delivery, physical expression, and deep emotional intensity.

Actively Doing & Acting in Movies

Transitioning from stage to screen, Vinay is actively doing and acting across major Telugu and Hindi feature films. Known for commanding screen presence, fierce eyes, and captivating dramatic realism, he specializes in high-stakes dramatic roles, intense character performances, and formidable antagonists.

Notable feature films include Saripodhaa Sanivaaram (2024 · as Martin), Ambajipeta Marriage Band (2024 · as Prasad), Dear Comrade (2019 · as Vinay), and Wild Dog (2021 · as Qateel).
